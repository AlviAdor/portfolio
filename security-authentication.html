Security Deep Dive

Verified before anything else happens.

Rate-limited logins, a CAPTCHA that can't be skipped by hiding it, sessions that rotate on every privilege change. Authentication here isn't a form that calls it a day once a password matches. It's enforced server-side, every time, the same way.

How it's enforced

Authentication, treated like a contract, not a checkbox.

Protection

Rate limiting

Brute-force attempts are throttled. Too many failed logins lock the account temporarily.

Sessions

Session integrity

Tokens are time-limited, rotation-safe, and cleared completely on logout.

Access

Role-based access

Each account type sees only what they are permitted to access.

The Conviction

I don't treat authentication as a feature—I treat it as a contract. If your account exists, it is defended. Every layer counts. I'll keep your session safe from the moment you log in until you log out.
