Password-gated deletion

Deleting a record (stock or customer) requires a password prompt, so a misclick during a busy shift can't silently wipe data.

Why a desktop app, not a web app

This one was built for a small mobile phone shop's actual day-to-day: adding new stock, logging sales, keeping a customer list, and not losing any of it to a crashed spreadsheet. A Windows Forms desktop app made more sense than a web app here, since the shop has one computer, no reliable internet dependency needed for basic operation, and the owner wanted something that opens and just works.

The feature I spent the most care on is the one that sounds the most minor: deletion is password-gated. Inventory and customer records are the kind of data where an accidental delete during a busy afternoon is a real problem, not a hypothetical one, so removing anything requires a deliberate password prompt rather than a single confirm dialog someone can click through on autopilot.

It's a C# / .NET project, and deliberately scoped to what the shop actually needed rather than a general-purpose POS system: add phones, manage customers, track stock, view records, protected deletion. No feature in it exists because it sounded impressive to build.
